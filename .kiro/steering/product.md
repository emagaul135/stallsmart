# Product — StallSmart

StallSmart is the offline-first AI bookkeeper for market vendors. A vendor pastes a supplier message; on-device AI parses it into structured stock records; sales are logged in two taps; a dashboard shows today's profit. Short jobs run on-device (Gemini Nano via Firebase AI Logic, free, private, offline); long jobs fall back to the cloud. Every AI answer shows an ON-DEVICE / IN-CLOUD badge so the hybrid behaviour is visible.

Audience: small market vendors worldwide, especially where mobile signal is bad. All UI copy in English; sample data in USD. The app must be a complete, coherent product — not a technical demo.
